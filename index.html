GPS Fieldmark

Check a GPS Fieldmark photo

Find out if a stamped photo is exactly as it was when it was taken. Your photo is checked here in your browser and is never uploaded.

How does this work?

When GPS Fieldmark saves a photo, the phone calculates a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the picture and signs it, together with the time, place and other details, using a key locked inside the phone's security chip. That signed record travels inside the photo file.

This page recalculates the fingerprint and checks the signature. If even one pixel or one detail was changed, the check fails.

What it can't tell you: who owns the phone, or that the scene in front of the camera was genuine. It shows that the file is unchanged since that phone stamped it, plus how the time and place were obtained (satellites, phone clock, typed in by hand, or added later to a gallery photo).

Sent on WhatsApp? WhatsApp and many apps shrink photos, which removes the record. Ask for the photo to be sent as a document (or by email / Drive) so the original file arrives unchanged.
